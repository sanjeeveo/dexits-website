Before you rely on an estimate

Check the evidence behind the number.

A useful estimate starts with reconciled revenue, complete costs and a clear reporting period. Young stores, incomplete imports and owner-entered margins carry additional uncertainty. Dexits does not certify financial statements or guarantee market value.

Questions

Know what to expect.

Is a Dexits valuation an offer to buy?

No. It is a model estimate. Buyers set their own offers after reviewing financial records and business risks.

Does Dexits provide a buyer marketplace or escrow?

The Shopify app provides valuation and exit preparation tools. It does not operate an in-app buyer marketplace or escrow service. Contact the team about any separate representation services.

Will installing a recommended app increase my value?

An installation alone does not establish business value. Any benefit needs to be supported by measured profit, reduced risk or better operating performance. Some recommendations contain affiliate links; Dexits may earn a commission if you buy through them.

Can an inactive or very young store be valued?

Limited recent trading or short history can make a profit-based estimate unreliable. Review the available evidence with a buyer or adviser; an estimate may be unavailable until the inputs are complete.

How do I contact Dexits?

Email info@emv.io or request a conversation. Contacting us does not list your store or commit you to a representation agreement.
